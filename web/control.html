HUD profile

How to set up a PSD

Name layers after what they show and the HUD keeps working with any design:

  • Text layers named after a live field (KILLS LEFT, GOLD RIGHT, TIMER, TRI-CODE LEFT, TURRETS LEFT, DRAGONS LEFT…, anything in Live fields) become live text in that layer's font, size, colour and alignment.
  • TEAM LEFT / TEAM RIGHT: filled with the team colour. TEAM LOGO LEFT: where the logo goes. BO3 1 LEFT, BO3 2 LEFT…: series pips.
  • Optional: LINES (tinted in the team colours), GOLD DIFF LEFT (where the gold-lead tab goes), OBJECTIVE TIMERS, DRAGONS ICON LEFT / HERALDS ICON LEFT / GRUBS ICON LEFT (hide with their counter).
  • Everything else is artwork. Fonts are found in Windows' fonts; any that aren't can be added here after importing.

Teams

Claimed objectives

Each team's count of dragons, Rift Heralds and void grubs taken, in the HUD. Switching one off hides its icon and number.

Match time

Jumps the League spectator client on this PC, and on every Observer Client connected to it, to 0:00 at the same moment. Needs League's Replay API: click "Turn on time control" once on each PC, then restart the League client.

Animations

When a team draws first blood, a stinger wipes across their side of the top bar, which fills with their colour and logo and "FIRST BLOOD", then wipes back (under 3 seconds). Preview plays it on the overlay now.

Webcams

Player cam link (players open it in Chrome or Edge on their PC)
How players connect their cams
  • In a browser: send players the link above. The first time, the browser warns that the connection isn't private (the HUD makes its own certificate): Advanced -> Continue. Then allow the camera and pick their team and role. Their cam goes straight to the HUD, peer to peer, for the least delay.
  • SCC Player Cam app: the same, as its own small app: SCC-Player-Cam-Setup.exe. Players enter this PC's address instead of using the link. No certificate warning, and it remembers their choices.
  • Both need "Let other PCs on the network read the HUD" ticked (Outputs) and the players on this network. Leave those players' link boxes blank.
  • Link: paste a VDO.Ninja view link (or any page showing just the cam) in the player's box. Works from anywhere, with a little more delay.
  • Who's on camera: with "Read this PC's screen" on, the cams follow the player name tags nearest the middle of the screen (Windows, League in Borderless or Windowed). Otherwise they follow the latest kill. Pin keeps a team's cam on one player.

Objective timers

Shown in the top-left of the overlay. A timer appears while its objective is counting down or LIVE, and drops off once it's gone for the game. Elder appears after a team takes soul. Switched-off timers are also left blank in the CSV.

Gold

Reads the gold and the dragon, herald and grub counters off the League window on this PC. Keep the spectator top bar showing. Falls back to the estimate below when it can't be read.

League's game client doesn't share other players' gold, so this is an estimate. If you see it running high or low against the in-game scoreboard, nudge the adjustment.
